## Projects

Here are some things you may enjoy! Many more can be found on my [GitHub](https://github.com/mooreryan/) page. (Though I can't promise you will actually enjoy them!)

[OCaml](https://ocaml.org/) is a nice language, and it's pretty fast as well!

- [pasv](https://github.com/mooreryan/pasv): Protein active site validation pipeline

- [Gleam qcheck](https://github.com/mooreryan/gleam_qcheck): QuickCheck-inspired property testing with integrated shrinking for Gleam
